Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

What we collect

  • When you buy: the payment provider (Razorpay in India, Gumroad elsewhere) collects your name, email, phone number where asked, and payment details. We receive your name, email, phone number (if given), country and order details. We never receive your full card, UPI or bank details.
  • When you email or message us: your message and contact details.
  • When you comment "TMAX" on Instagram or Facebook: Meta sends you an automatic message with the purchase links. Meta's own privacy policy covers that conversation.
  • This website: we use no cookies, analytics or ads. Our host, GitHub Pages, may log visitors' IP addresses for security.

How we use it

Only to deliver your ebook, answer your questions, handle refunds or payment problems, and keep records the law requires. We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not send marketing email unless you ask for it.

Sharing

We share data only with the services that run the purchase (Razorpay, Gumroad) or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed for orders, support and legal or tax requirements. Messages are deleted when no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by emailing aethericchemist@gmail.com. Some order records may have to be kept by law.
