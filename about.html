About Aethericchemist

Aethericchemist is a men's health education brand based in Chennai, India. We publish plain-language guides on the everyday habits that research links to healthy testosterone: sleep, diet, training, stress and lab testing.

Our main product is TMAX, a downloadable PDF ebook. On Instagram (@aethericchemist) we post short summaries of published research.

How we work

  • Studies we cite must exist on PubMed, and we link to them.
  • We do not promise results, publish testimonials or show before-and-after claims.
  • Our content is education, not medical advice. For diagnosis or treatment, see a doctor.
